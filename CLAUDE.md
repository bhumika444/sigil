# Sigil — Know Your Agent (KYA)

Hackathon project: AI for Finance (Lovable x n8n x MiniMax)

## What
Cryptographic identity layer for AI agents in financial transactions. The "KYC for agents" — verified, portable, tamper-proof agent identities.

## Key Context
- Research brief: `KYA_System_Hackathon_Research.md`
- Gap: Everyone is building payment rails for AI agents, nobody has solved the identity layer
- Target: The 96:1 ratio of non-human to human identities in finance, none with verified financial identity
